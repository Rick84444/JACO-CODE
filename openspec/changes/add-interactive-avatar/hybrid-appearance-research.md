## Research sources and transfer
- SplattingAvatar (CVPR 2024), https://initialneil.github.io/SplattingAvatar.html : triangle-bound Gaussians animated by underlying mesh. Authors report >300 FPS RTX 3090 / 30 FPS iPhone 13 on their own workloads; monocular video, not a single still. Commercial use of upstream code requires permission per https://github.com/initialneil/SplattingAvatar. Architecture reference only.
- SqueezeMe, https://forresti.github.io/squeezeme/ : compresses pose-dependent corrections, reports three avatars at 72 FPS on Quest 3 with custom Vulkan. Not a browser benchmark. Instant Skinned Gaussian Avatars https://gaussian-vrm.github.io/ and https://github.com/naruya/gaussian-vrm : local scan-to-skinned-splats; source MIT, sample rights separate; genuine scan input, not single photo reconstruction. HAHA https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/ACCV2024/html/Svitov_HAHA_Highly_Articulated_Gaussian_Human_Avatars_with_Textured_Mesh_Prior_ACCV_2024_paper.html : selective Gaussian appearance over textured mesh, particularly hair/clothes. Research architecture only; dependent SMPL-X rights not cleared. LHM++ weights CC BY-NC per https://github.com/aigc3d/LHM-plusplus/blob/main/LICENSE_WEIGHT; do not ship.
- Spark https://github.com/sparkjsdev/spark : MIT renderer with documented skinning, plausible Three.js integration; peer-version compatibility does not prove runtime functionality. Pin and audit actual released version before adoption. Bone skinning alone cannot transport expression morphs. SPZ https://github.com/nianticlabs/spz : MIT compressed splat representation, not a rig or face reconstruction method.
- Epic Games MetaHuman rig https://dev.epicgames.com/documentation/metahuman/metahuman-dna-rig-definition-and-rig-operation and LOD https://dev.epicgames.com/documentation/metahuman/platform-support-and-lod-specifications-for-metahumans : separate identity, joints, expression shapes, corrective deformation, synchronized LOD. https://dev.epicgames.com/documentation/metahuman/controlling-metahuman-levels-of-detail-lods : hair cards at lower quality levels. Adopt principles, not copied assets. https://github.com/EpicGames/OpenRigLogic is MIT C++/Python rig evaluation, not automatic face authoring or browser integration. https://www.metahuman.com/license states cross-engine use allowed and standard Unreal terms apply; old DNA Calibration and sample asset terms differ. Audit exact version, redistribution and use restrictions (including AI-related clauses) before any asset reuse; no license clearance inferred from MIT library. 
- Naughty Dog SIGGRAPH 2016, Yibing Jiang, The Process of Creating Volumetric-based Materials in Uncharted 4, https://advances.realtimerendering.com/s2016/index.html : precomputed self-shadowing, approximate material scattering, high-resolution surface detail and selective shading simplification. Transfer through original shaders and controlled visual tests, not game assets.
- Unity Enemies/The Heretic https://github.com/Unity-Technologies/com.unity.demoteam.digital-human : eye/skin/teeth/hair shaders, deformation tech. Unity-dependent licensing, sample art explicitly educational/noncommercial https://github.com/Unity-Technologies/com.unity.demoteam.digital-human.sample/blob/master/LICENSE.md. Study principles, do not port licensed code/assets without clearance. Hair system https://github.com/Unity-Technologies/com.unity.demoteam.hair : attached strand roots and LOD/simulation inspiration. SIGGRAPH 2025 https://advances.realtimerendering.com/s2025/index.html : shipped strand-based hair in Indiana Jones at 60Hz, platform-specific engineering, not equivalent to WebGL.
- Khronos glTF compressor https://github.com/KhronosGroup/glTF-Compressor : KTX2, meshopt and other geometry compression. https://github.com/KhronosGroup/glTF-Sample-Assets/tree/main/Models/MorphStressTest : regression fixture for active morph capacity, audit per-asset license. Three physical material https://threejs.org/docs/pages/MeshPhysicalMaterial.html : selective anisotropy, sheen, transmission, per-pixel cost. Prefer controlled baked approximations on constrained devices.

## Hypotheses and production decisions
H0: anatomically fitted, well-textured mesh is sufficient at actual presentation framing. H1: same rig plus selective splats (hair first or difficult region only) improves held-out visual quality within resource budget. H2: full-body mesh-bound Gaussian appearance is worth higher complexity only if H1 fails quality and H2 passes resource tests. Splats sampled from a low-detail mesh cannot restore missing photographic information. Do not equate synthetic novel views to measured scans. Do not replace current avatar with a billboard or demo. No engine migration authorized.
Single canonical skeleton, expression state and audio clock. Independent surface geometry for eyes/cornea, teeth, tongue, opaque garment, scalp and skin. Use pose-corrective morphs for shoulder, neck, elbow and blink/jaw, and preserve UV and expression semantics through LOD. Face textures: de-lit albedo, normal, roughness and selective subsurface approximation. Eyes: real spherical geometry with plausible cornea, iris depth, tear line, controlled reflections; test eyelid closure. Hair: artist-authored guide curves to layered cards, alpha coverage and anisotropic highlight; limited secondary bones and collision envelope. Compare with calibrated splats, not random point decorations. Mesh-bound splats attach by triangle ID, barycentric coords and local tangent frame; transform orientation/covariance and offset with the deformed triangle including face morphs; reject degeneracy. 
Use signal-driven behavior layer: gaze targets, saccades, blink, breathing, audio-clock-driven visemes, coarticulation, interruptible gestures and smooth transitions. No invented live speech timestamps. 
## Acceptance
Compare paired same-camera A/B at actual full-body size, medium portrait and closeup plus held-out side angle; keep reference identity and lighting fixed. Score independent operator visual review on identity 30%, face/mouth motion 25%, body 20%, hair/clothes 15%, multi-view 10%; target >=8/10 and no critical region below 7. Technical gates independent: complete blink/bilabial closure without intersection, natural coarticulation from 20 Swedish and 10 English utterances, audio alignment target <=150ms, no foot slide on planted clips, no visible skin through outfit, repeated playback and interruption. LOD comparison with facial controls retained where visible; test low tier separately. Real Windows and Android browsers: >=24 and >=15 median FPS respectively, p95 frame time <=67ms where feasible, local model throughput loss <=5% vs avatar off, no hidden-tab rendering, no unbounded memory. Record actual hardware, render dimensions, GPU and memory estimates, draw calls, exact commit and assets. These are targets, not observed measurements. 
Next implementation: rights/provenance and real anatomy/identity surface authored first; establish material/eye/closed-lid and hair baseline before adding new renderer. Evaluate exact same asset on mesh and selective splat routes only when source appearance is sufficiently detailed. Use existing runtime in separate scoped integration work pending Codex coordination. No installation, model generation, implementation tests or merge in this research commit.
