# Aura V31 facial speech experiment
Baseline: existing FFT heuristic drives only four vowel morphs, uses frame-count smoothing, and cannot capture audio on browsers lacking captureStream. Hypothesis: an actual decoded PCM timeline using a small pinned MFCC classifier and all 14 V31 visemes improves perceived lip closure and synchronization without changing model, camera or voice. HeadAudio upstream revision is recorded in vendor NOTICE. Competing approaches: provider phoneme timestamps (preferred when actually available), TalkingHead language text modules (no verified Swedish module), Rhubarb offline executable (extra native dependency). No new network service. No claim of Swedish phoneme accuracy: English model transfer is experimental. 
Acceptance: all 14 shapes bounded, PP closure precedence, explicit silence, media-time seek and cancellation, equal smoothing at different FPS, no audio output interruption; actual Swedish and English held-out utterances with human ratings, frame-by-frame alignment and actual V31 geometry on Windows/Android are required before production. Aim: audible/visible onset error below 100 ms in 90% of measured voiced segments, silence closure, no lip/teeth intersections, no audible dropouts. Unit tests establish contracts, not lip-reading accuracy.
