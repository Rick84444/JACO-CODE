# Aura conversational behavior — production requirements
2026-11-27. Operator requirement: natural movements during questions and answers, not canned game-character animation. Extend the canonical facial/body behavior engine; no separate agent, runtime, sensor permission or per-frame language model call. Preserve current identity and resource budgets.

## Research basis
- Skantze, Turn-taking in conversational systems and human-robot interaction (2021), https://doi.org/10.1016/j.csl.2020.101178 — multimodal turn-taking, speech/prosody, gaze, breath and gesture; use coordinated cues, not fixed animation sequences.
- Holler et al., Processing language in face-to-face conversation: Questions with gestures get faster responses, https://doi.org/10.3758/s13423-017-1363-z — observed relationship between visual cues and response timing; not a universal timing prescription.
- Axelsson et al., Modeling Feedback in Interaction With Conversational Agents—A Review, https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/computer-science/articles/10.3389/fcomp.2022.744574/full — listener backchannels and their context-dependent communicative functions. Listening acknowledgement must not imply agreement, comprehension or verified success.
- Turn-taking in human face-to-face interaction is multimodal, https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9985971/ — coordinated gaze and hand gesture at transitions. All sources inform behavior hypotheses, not proven Aura quality.

## Behavior contract
Orthogonal conversation floor (idle, receptive, user speaking, end-of-turn uncertain, awaiting answer, preparing speech, speaking, yielding, interrupted) and mission state. Use observed playback/start/end/cancel events and available speech activity or text submission. No invented user facial expression, physical gaze target, emotion, comprehension or tool outcome. Camera-facing attention is virtual screen direction unless actual authorized tracking exists. No extra camera/mic access. Text-only questions use submission, not simulated audio activity.
Use bounded speech act/gesture hints only if actually produced by response pipeline or safe deterministic derivation: question, explanation, contrast, enumeration, confirmed result, uncertainty, clarification. Hints optional; neutral behavior when ambiguous. Never use gesture to exaggerate certainty or imply agreement. Keep warm but professional baseline. 
Listening: relaxed orientation, stable gaze with natural breaks, sparse low-amplitude acknowledgement around actual pauses; avoid nodding during every sentence or every question. Analysis: subtle attention shift only while actually processing, not obligatory thinking performance. Preparing answer: ease into pose as playback becomes ready, no artificial wait. Answer: semantic beat accents near relevant timed phrase only if timing exists; articulation dominates expressive mouth targets. Question: optional slight brow/tilt and gaze invitation, yield on actual speech end. Clarification: attentive neutral expression. Long tasks: varied low-amplitude quiet posture, no fake activity loops. Interrupt: cancel old utterance cues using utterance IDs, ease gesture to stop/hold/retract, release mouth when playback stops and transition to observed listening state. Do not interrupt an unrelated mission merely because dialogue changes.
Blend independent gaze, lids, face, neck, torso and hands; maintain pose continuity and planted feet; bounded time-based dynamics, gesture refractory periods and neutral fallback. No reset to T-pose or repeated identical gesture for each question. Hands can remain still. Reduced motion and hidden tabs obey current policy. 
## Verification

Scenarios: short factual question, complex technical question, follow-up, yes/no, clarification, enumeration, disagreement, long compute wait, actual audio interruption and resume, rapid alternating turns, text-only, failed synthesis, simultaneous background mission, reduced motion and hidden tab. Check no talk motion without actual playback or explicitly approximate supported signal, no false agreement, no old utterance gesture after cancellation, no extra delay introduced, no stale callbacks, posture continuity. Log timestamps and compare actual playback and dialogue events, measure interruption transition and timing, inspect video at full body and closeup. Independent operator review of naturalness, appropriateness, repetition and facial/body continuity. Compare real-device throughput and frame time with existing resource gates; no claimed benchmark before measurement.
