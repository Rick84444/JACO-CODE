# Code of conduct

We want JACO to be a place where people can work on hard problems without being worn down.

**Expected:** be respectful and direct; assume good faith; criticise the work, not the person; give credit; say what you know and what you only believe; accept a "no" on scope gracefully.

**Not accepted:** harassment, insults or discrimination of any kind; personal attacks; publishing someone's private information; sexualised language or attention; sustained disruption of discussions; deliberately misleading claims about what code does or what was tested.

**Scope:** this applies in issues, pull requests, discussions and any other space where someone represents the project.

**Enforcement:** maintainers may edit or remove comments, close threads, and block people who break these rules. Report a problem to `hello@jacocode.com`; reports are kept private. The maintainers' decision is final, but you can ask for it to be looked at again.

This text is short on purpose. It is inspired by the Contributor Covenant (https://www.contributor-covenant.org/), which you are welcome to read for fuller examples.
