Privacy

This page describes what jacocode.com does with your data. It is short because there is little of it.

Signing in with Google

If you choose Sign in with Google, Google tells us your Google account id, your email address and your name. Nothing else is requested (the scopes are openid, email and profile).

We keep these in one cookie in your browser, signed so it cannot be altered, valid for 30 days. We do not store them in a database and we do not share them. Signing out deletes the cookie.

Visiting the site

The site sets no cookies for visitors who do not sign in, loads no advertising or analytics and no fonts or scripts from other sites. The hosting provider, Cloudflare, sees ordinary request logs.

JACO Lite

The Lite download runs on your computer. It sends nothing to us. It sends your requests to the model provider that you connect, and only to that one.
