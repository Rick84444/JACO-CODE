# Aura production facial performance research
2026-11-26. Operator focus: real human-like facial motion, speech articulation, eyes and fine detail, not a demo. Keep approved portrait identity and one canonical Aura runtime. Research only; no new renderer, paid service, permissions or model execution.

## Inspected baseline
Branch head ca80dc732a8ca2eb8c9ec72197bc4ecd53a71126. runtime/src/lib/auraRigLightform.mjs createBehaviorEngine uses jaw and four vowel morph channels (aa/O/E/U); M/B/P have low jaw rather than explicit lip closure, F/V share approximate vowel mixture. Audio FFT is an approximation, numeric level and speaking-only fallbacks synthesize periodic mouth shapes. Renderer applies per-frame constant smoothing factors, not frame-rate-invariant dynamics. Blink schedule uses t % 5.3 with two fixed pulses. Gaze is partly derived from yaw and micro-saccade signal. AuraPortrait.tsx synthesizes speech via /api/aura/voice/speak and taps actual playback with captureStream; no explicit phoneme/viseme timing delivery was found in that component. Existing V4 local GLB showed incomplete blink at full target; not a diagnosis of underlying topology. Do not equate 155 morph target instances with 155 independent calibrated facial controls.

## Research and transfer
- JALI (SIGGRAPH 2016) https://www.dgp.toronto.edu/~elf/jali.html separates jaw and lip articulation, uses acoustic and linguistic information; adopt principles, not unreviewed code or assets. SynFace https://link.springer.com/article/10.1155/2009/191940 provides low-latency coarticulated articulatory control and a precedent for mobile speech-driven faces. Swedish vowel study https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13585966/ supports quantitative mouth geometry rather than oversimplified rounding/openness classes; calibrate Swedish /y/, /ʉ/, /u/, /ø/ independently, including context. Do not assume English Oculus visemes distinguish all Swedish phonemes.
- Microsoft documentation https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/azure/ai-services/speech-service/language-support documents sv-SE viseme IDs (not necessarily Swedish full-face blendshape streams); https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/azure/ai-services/speech-service/how-to-speech-synthesis-viseme documents audio offsets; provider-dependent option only if already configured/authorized, not a requirement or purchase. Swedish MFA models (see anatomical-fit-research.md) for offline timing reference, not streaming. HeadAudio https://github.com/met4citizen/HeadAudio provides browser AudioWorklet candidate, example English model; evaluate Swedish before adopting, audit model and code licenses. HeadTTS English only per current README. NVIDIA https://github.com/NVIDIA/Audio2Face-3D and https://github.com/NVIDIA/Audio2Face-3D-SDK/blob/main/docs/README.md: high-fidelity offline comparison candidate, SDK MIT but separate model terms and CUDA/TensorRT requirements. No assumption of available hardware or Swedish quality. Epic https://dev.epicgames.com/documentation/en-us/metahuman/audio-driven-animation distinguishes mouth-only and full-face curves; use separation principle.
- Pejsa et al. https://graphics.cs.wisc.edu/Papers/2015/PAGM15/ and https://graphics.cs.wisc.edu/Papers/2012/APMG12a/ coordinate gaze targets with eye/head/upper body and stabilize gaze as head moves. Eyelid kinematics https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1002/cav.354. Blink physiology https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12358864/ distinguishes full voluntary closure from partial spontaneous blinks; full closure must be possible, not every natural blink identical. Bilateral temporal coupling https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/7928197/. Do not use a fixed repeating blink cycle or arbitrary jitter.
- Epic eye materials https://dev.epicgames.com/documentation/metahuman/eye-material-tools and https://dev.epicgames.com/documentation/en-us/unreal-engine/digital-humans?application_version=4.27 distinguish iris/pupil/cornea/sclera and refraction. Unity digital human project https://github.com/Unity-Technologies/com.unity.demoteam.digital-human uses specialized tearline/eye/skin/teeth shading; study only, no licensed code or assets copied.

## Proposed implementation order
1. Inspect actual mesh controls and topology. Map names to anatomical channels, validate nonzero deltas and correct coordinated skin/lash/teeth/tongue deformation. Repair identity-specific full blink and lip seal geometry, eye look extremes, jaw pivot and teeth contacts first. Full blink occludes iris in frontal and oblique renders without eye penetration, paired lips touch along intended seam for /m b p/ and no teeth through lip. Maintain eyelid following vertical gaze. Avoid double-driving jaw by bone and morph. Preserve facial identity through corrections. 
2. Add time-stamped articulation input to existing speech playback path: utterance ID, timebase seconds, monotonic start/end, explicit silence, source and confidence. Use actual audio playback clock (including pause/resume, seek, chunk offsets, cancellation). Discard stale events; no fake timestamps, no simulated cadence as successful speech. Priority provider timings > verified local detection > explicitly approximate audio envelope > neutral. Unknown shapes stay bounded. No extra inference per render frame.
3. Derive compact control vector for jaw rotation/translation, lip closure, upper/lower lip roll, protrusion, rounding, corner stretch, teeth contact, tongue positions, cheeks. Map to supported viseme/muscle controls only; missing controls produce asset capability failure not invented output. Coarticulation with context-dependent anticipation/carryover, closure constraints and time-based interpolation. Compare full timeline vs 60–120 ms bounded lookahead as experimental setting, not physiological universal. No artificial audio delay without measuring total conversational latency. Prosody and expression layers independently bounded, articulation constraints dominate smile/lip conflict.
4. Eye behavior: select target from actual existing mission/listening/speaking events, explicit camera/screen target when known, no false gaze into unobserved user environment. Stable fixations, amplitude-dependent saccades, slower coordinated head turn and counter-rotation during head settling; binocular convergence to shared target, clamp extremes. Seeded variable blink event timing, partial/full events with distinct closing/opening trajectories, coupled eyes with limited asymmetry. No independent random twitch per frame. Use actual dt, test 15/24/60 FPS and long frame stalls. Stop rendering when hidden, obey reduced-motion. 
5. Eye materials: blue-green radial iris variation and limbal ring; nonuniform sclera, distinct corneal highlight/refraction approximation, subtle tearline/occlusion, eyelashes attached to lids, physically coherent illumination. Pupil reacts to scene illumination, not fabricated emotion. Compare low-cost baked/material version against more costly shader at matched distance. Mouth wetness and enamel use separate materials; wrinkles around lips, nose and eyes driven by actual deformation, not random overlays.

## Falsifiable acceptance
Compare same V4-derived identity, lighting and speech against existing renderer baseline. 20 held-out Swedish + 10 English utterances, with recorded timing oracle/manual annotations independent of animation engine; include M/B/P closure, F/V tooth contact, rounded vowel contrasts, silence, fast speech, interrupt and resume. Measure absolute/signed audio-visual event error, median/p95, audio/animation start latency and closure recall; proposed target median <=40ms, p95 <=80ms for annotated salient events where ground truth supports it, report uncertainty and device frame quantization. Full closure test independent of natural partial blinks; sample frontal/45/90 and gaze extrema, no visible iris during intentional full closure, no lid/cornea clipping, no lip/teeth intersection; record images and video. Negative controls: shifted audio, missing lip seal and frozen eye, periodic blink baseline, malformed or stale timing. Independent operator review blind paired clips, speech and listening, actual presentation scale and closeup; judge likeness, eye naturalness, articulation, continuity, not number of morphs. Maintain prior >=8/10 overall target and no critical region below 7; Windows/Android median >=24/15 FPS and local inference throughput loss <=5% on real devices, targets not measurements.
